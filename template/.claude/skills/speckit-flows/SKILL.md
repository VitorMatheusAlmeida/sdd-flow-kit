## User Input

```text
$ARGUMENTS
```

Explicit mode, if given: `create`, `sync`, `tasks` or `validate`. With no argument, run **auto** (Step 1). Any other user text is context and MUST be considered.

## Language

This skill and its tooling are in English. **Everything it generates is in `project.locale` of `sdd.config.json`** (default `pt-BR`, the language of the bundled template): scripts (`roteiros/`), their index, draw.io labels, and any text written into spec, plan or tasks. The validator reads the script sections "Caminho feliz" / "Happy path" and "Estresse" / "Stress"; a translated `roteiro-template.md` must keep one of these titles.

## What this skill guarantees

Every feature has, next to `spec.md` and `plan.md`:

```text
specs/<feature>/
├── fluxos.drawio          # DETAILED decision flowchart, one page per flow — the owner edits it
└── roteiros/
    ├── README.md          # index: R##, journey, actors, US/FR, status of the 3 axes
    └── R##-<name>.md      # walkthrough script (template: .specify/templates/roteiro-template.md)
```

- **The draw.io is the source of the flow** once created. The script is its executable summary: the ASCII drawing summarizes; the draw.io carries every **input**, every **action** (by the user and by the system), every **decision** with labeled exits, every **error** with the exact message, and every **external effect** (e-mail, another role).
- **The spec is the source of intent.** When the draw.io diverges from the spec, the divergence is the **owner's decision**: point it out and ask; never rewrite `spec.md` or `plan.md` on your own.
- **Walkthrough** (passagem) is the execution of the script by the `flow-walkthrough` skill (18 points, matrix, second round). The script is born in the plan with everything ⏳; the walkthrough fills in the status. The `R##` of a script is the flow id of the walkthrough (`<FEATURE_DIR>/<walkthrough.outputDir>/flows.json`).
- **Generate complete, not provisional.** A spec is usually born and not modified: the flow is generated in full from it, not detailed in waves or left waiting for changes. A change of direction comes as a new spec, or as an explicit edit by the owner.
- **History lives in git.** There are no copies, logs or snapshots of the flow: the base for "what changed" is the last commit of `fluxos.drawio`, and each absorbed change is committed (by the owner, or with the owner's permission).

draw.io conventions (kinds, colors, ids, the single-page process map, source format): [`reference/drawio.md`](reference/drawio.md).
Script: `node .specify/scripts/node/flows-drawio.mjs` (`generate`, `text`, `diff`, `validate`). **The script only reads and lists.** Understanding a change and deciding whether it is valid is your job — never apply a change because the script listed it.

## Step 1 — Context and mode

1. Run the prerequisites script of the installed spec-kit variant from the repo root — `.specify/scripts/powershell/check-prerequisites.ps1 -Json -PathsOnly` (or `.specify/scripts/bash/check-prerequisites.sh --json --paths-only`) — and take `FEATURE_DIR`. Read `sdd.config.json` (repository root) for `project.locale`, `walkthrough.outputDir` and `accounts.roles`; without it, generate in pt-BR and say so. Set `DRAWIO = FEATURE_DIR/fluxos.drawio`, `ROTEIROS = FEATURE_DIR/roteiros`, `TASKS = FEATURE_DIR/tasks.md`.
2. No `spec.md`: STOP and point to `/speckit-specify`. No `plan.md` in `create` mode: STOP and point to `/speckit-plan`.
3. Auto mode, in this order (each step runs if its condition holds; several can run in one call):
   1. `DRAWIO` does not exist → **create**.
   2. `DRAWIO` exists, is committed, and `git status` shows it modified → **sync**.
   3. Always → **validate**.
   4. `TASKS` exists → **tasks**.

## Mode create

1. **Read** `spec.md` (user stories, acceptance scenarios, FRs, edge cases, out of scope), `plan.md`, `contracts/`, `data-model.md` and `quickstart.md` when present.
2. **Cut the flows.** A flow (R##) is an **end-to-end journey of a user**, not a screen or a route: it starts at a trigger (someone opens a screen, an e-mail arrives) and ends at an observable result. A story may become several flows and a flow may cover several stories. Number `R01`, `R02`… in the order they happen in the product. Every acceptance scenario and edge case MUST land in at least one flow (happy path, stress or access). If one does not fit, list it and ask.
3. **Write the source** as JSON, **outside the repository** (`$env:TEMP\speckit-flows\<feature>.json`), as a **process map**: one page, `layout: "horizontal"`, every flow in it as a zone, in the format of [`reference/drawio.md`](reference/drawio.md). It is the contract the front builds the screens from, so it comes from what the spec agreed — never from the screens the front has today — and no detail is spared. Mandatory detail (labels in pt-BR):
   - the happy path of the whole feature runs left to right on row 0, flow after flow; side journeys are bands above or below, linked to the main row by their real trigger;
   - every screen (`screen`) the person passes through, with its title on the shape and, in the caption, what it shows, **every field** (name, required or optional, the options it offers, where its value comes from) and every button with its on-screen text — format and validation rules (size, digits, pattern, uniqueness) do **not** go on the screen: they belong to the contract and show up in the verification step that follows, as the cases of its error;
   - who acts is the actor tag on each shape (no lanes);
   - every action (`action`): the button, or what the system does (stores, sends, computes, blocks);
   - every decision (`decision`) as a short question, with **all** exits labeled;
   - checks in a row that lead to the same place are **one** decision, and their failures **one** error whose caption lists every case with its message (`E#` per decision, one stress row per case);
   - every error (`error`) one row below its decision, offset half a column to the right, with `E#`, the messages and where they show; when the person goes back to an earlier step, a reference card (`reference`, "↩ Volta para <etapa>") further down the same diagonal — never a long line back;
   - every external effect (`external`) one row above, with to whom and what it carries; variants (`variant`) above or below; a hand-off to another flow far away is a reference card ("→ Segue no R##");
   - branches spread on both sides of the main row like a snowflake (errors and references down, e-mails and variants up); no zone frames, no symbol key; connectors in draw.io's default style (orthogonal, square corners);
   - rules that do not fit a shape (`note`), placed next to what they explain, starting with "Regra:";
   - captions outside the shapes, short lines broken at phrase boundaries, labelled ("Campos:", "Mensagem:", "Botões:", "Regra:");
   - every acceptance scenario and edge case of the spec lands somewhere on the map.
     Before showing it, check that no connector crosses a shape or a caption and that no shape overlaps another.
4. **Generate** `node .specify/scripts/node/flows-drawio.mjs generate <source.json> <DRAWIO>`. Discard the source afterwards: from now on the draw.io is the source, and two sources are forbidden.
5. **Write the scripts** from the template (`.specify/scripts/powershell/resolve-template.ps1 roteiro-template`): one `R##-<name>.md` per flow, in pt-BR, with the ASCII drawing matching nodes `R##-P##` and `R##-E##`, the "Esperado" that spec and contracts promise, and everything ⏳. An error message the spec does not define: write the proposal and mark it `(a confirmar)`. Write `roteiros/README.md` with the index and the status legend.
6. **Validate** (validate mode) and fix until it passes.
7. Show the owner the list of flows (R##, journey, actors, US/FR covered) and the `(a confirmar)` items, and tell them the generated files need a commit before any edit, so that git has the base to compare with.

## Mode sync — the owner edited the draw.io

1. **Read what changed:** `node .specify/scripts/node/flows-drawio.mjs diff <DRAWIO>` (compares with `HEAD`; pass another ref as the second argument when the base is older) and, for context, `… text <DRAWIO>`. The list is raw material, not a verdict.
2. **Understand each change** — what it means for the product, in pt-BR and without jargon: new step, new decision, new exit, new error or different message, new input or different rule, different role, removed step. Read the surrounding nodes, the spec and the code when needed: a moved edge may change the meaning of a whole path.
3. **Judge whether each change is valid.** A change is valid when it is coherent, not merely well drawn. Check, for each one:
   - **The flow still works:** no node without an exit, no decision without all exits, no path that never reaches an end, no dead end for the user.
   - **The spec still holds:** the change fits an existing story or FR — or it is a change of intent, which only the owner decides.
   - **Other flows still hold:** a step removed here may be the trigger of another R##; a new rule here may contradict one there.
   - **Contracts and tables still hold:** a new input needs a field in the route and a column; a removed one may leave orphans. Compare with `contracts/` and `tabelas.planejado.dbml`.
   - **The skills still hold:** the change does not ask for a rule that only lives on the screen, a table read by a module that is not its owner, or a message with a technical id.
   - **Ambiguity:** a label that can be read two ways is a question, not a change.
4. **Report the evaluation**, one line per change:

   | Mudança no fluxo | Válida? | Por quê | Roteiro (linha) | Spec (US/AC/FR) | Plano / contrato / tabelas | Tarefas / código |
   | ---------------- | ------- | ------- | --------------- | --------------- | -------------------------- | ---------------- |

   Read the code when the question is "does this already exist?". Do not invent impact: if you do not know, say so.

5. **Apply only what is valid and derived**, without asking: scripts (ASCII drawing, happy path and stress rows, new `E#`), the index, and the ids of new nodes in the `.drawio` (change the `id` in the `<object>`/`<mxCell>` and in the edges pointing to it, keeping position and style).
6. **Ask before** anything that is not valid as drawn (propose the fix) and before any change to `spec.md`, `plan.md`, `contracts/`, `data-model.md` or the tables: that is intent, and the decision belongs to the owner. With the answer, apply it (or point to `/speckit-clarify` when the change opens ambiguities). New tasks for the code: point to `/speckit-converge`, which already runs this sync first.
7. **Validate.** When everything is absorbed, say so and suggest the commit that closes the change (message in Conventional Commits, pt-BR); do not commit without the owner's permission.

## Mode tasks — one walkthrough per script

1. Read `TASKS`. For each `roteiros/R##-*.md`, look for a task containing `Passagem do R##`.
2. If any is missing, **append at the end** (never rewrite, renumber or delete existing tasks) the section `## Phase N: Passagem dos fluxos` (N = highest phase + 1; ids from the highest `T###` + 1), with, in pt-BR:
   - `- [ ] T### [US#] Passagem do R## — <jornada> com a skill flow-walkthrough (18 pontos, caminho feliz, estresse, acesso, visual e ausências) em specs/<feature>/roteiros/R##-<nome>.md; matriz em specs/<feature>/<walkthrough.outputDir>/matrix-R##.md` — one per script, in the dependency order of the stories it covers;
   - before the first walkthrough task, once: `- [ ] T### Preparar a passagem: entrada de cada R## em specs/<feature>/<walkthrough.outputDir>/flows.json e cenários com as contas de accounts.roles (sdd.config.json)`;
   - `- [ ] T### Segunda volta de todos os R## até nenhum achado novo`;
   - `- [ ] T### Fechar os fluxos: /speckit-flows validate sem divergência e índice dos roteiros em dia`.
3. All present: do not touch `TASKS`.

## Mode validate

`node .specify/scripts/node/flows-drawio.mjs validate <DRAWIO> <ROTEIROS>`. It checks: every `①` step has node `R##-P##` and vice versa; every `E#` has node `R##-E##` and vice versa; every R## page has a script and vice versa; nodes with no incoming edge or no exit; decisions with fewer than two exits or an unlabeled exit; nodes without a flow id. A divergence is fixed in the script (derived) or in the draw.io when it is an obvious drawing error (dangling edge); a divergence of intent goes to the owner.

## Report

In pt-BR, short: mode(s) that ran, files created or changed, the draw.io changes with their evaluation (valid or not, and why), impact, open questions and the next command. Do not repeat the scripts' content in the conversation.

## Rules
